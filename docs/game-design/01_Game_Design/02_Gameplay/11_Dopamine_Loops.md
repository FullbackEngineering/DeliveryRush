# 11 — Dopamine Loops: Delivery Rush

## Overview
Delivery Rush reward loops are *skill-based and transparent*, not luck-based or manipulative. Dopamine spikes from:
1. **Anticipation** of the next delivery (will I keep the combo alive?).
2. **Mastery feedback** (clean execution = higher score/combo).
3. **Combo building** (chaining deliveries, watching multiplier grow to 8×).

No loot boxes, no daily streaks, no FOMO language. Every reward acknowledges player agency and timing skill.

---

## Loop Hierarchy

### Micro-Loop: Action Feedback (Every 2–5 seconds)
**Action:** Player holds gas, taps steer, or reaches beacon.

**Trigger:**  
- Holding gas: Engine rumble synth (frequency changes with speed).
- Tapping steer: Satisfying "beep" and visual flash (car pivots smoothly).
- Reaching beacon: Chime sound, beacon glows brighter.

**Reward:** Purely sensory. No points yet, just confirmation that the action worked.

**Frequency:** Constant during gameplay.

**Dopamine release:** Minimal but continuous. Keeps the player feeling active and responsive.

**Health:** Very healthy. These are *confirming* inputs, not slot-machine pulls.

---

### Minute-Loop: Delivery Completion (Every 30–45 seconds; 2–3 per run)
**Action:** Player completes a delivery (reaches dropoff beacon within time limit).

**Trigger:** Dropoff reached → time meter stops → score calculated.

**Reveal (1–2 seconds):**  
Score breakdown appears:
- Base: 100 coins
- Distance bonus: 0–80 (depends on path efficiency)
- Clean delivery: +100 coins (crash or near-miss = 0)
- Combo multiplier: ×1 to ×8 (based on clean-delivery streak)

**Example:** Delivery 3 with ×3 combo, clean drive = **(100 + 50 + 100) × 3 = 750 coins**.

**Feedback (2–3 seconds):** Confetti burst, triumphant chime (pitch tied to score size—higher score = higher pitch). Combo counter increments visibly.

**Integration (5–10 seconds):** Combo counter displayed prominently. Next delivery beacon appears, building anticipation. Player now thinks: "If I get 4 clean deliveries in a row, I hit 4×… if I get 5, I'm at 5×."

**Frequency:** Every delivery (typically every 30–45s in-run).

**Dopamine release:** Peak. Spike happens at the *reveal* (seeing the combo number increment), not the final score. Anticipation sustains through the next delivery attempt.

**Health:** Extremely healthy. Dopamine is tied to *player skill* (clean delivery = higher combo), not randomness. The combo multiplier itself is the mechanic that creates anticipation: "Can I keep it alive?"

---

### Session-Loop: Run Completion (Once per 45–90 second run)
**Action:** Countdown clock hits 0:00 (end of run).

**Trigger:** Run ends. Results screen appears.

**Reveal (3–5 seconds):** Results show:
- **Total score:** Sum of all deliveries' rewards.
- **Best combo:** Highest multiplier reached in this run (e.g., "Best combo: ×5").
- **Total coins:** Currency earned.
- **Run summary:** Number of successful deliveries, distance traveled.

**Feedback (3–5 seconds):** Jingle/music swell. If score > personal best, a "NEW RECORD!" banner appears with fanfare. If combo > 4×, a "Delivery Master" badge may appear (optional cosmetic unlock).

**Integration (5–10 seconds):** Buttons appear: "Restart," "Garage" (to view/equip vehicle and cards), "Results" (to see detailed breakdown). Player chooses to retry or visit the garage.

**Frequency:** Once per run (instant restart available).

**Dopamine release:** Strong but brief. The big dopamine *anticipation* comes *during* the run (chasing combo). The results screen *confirms* success.

**Health:** Healthy. No punishment for not achieving a high score. No "limited time" cosmetics. Results are purely informational.

---

### Long-Loop: Profile Progression (Across multiple runs)
**Action:** Accumulating coins across runs → unlocking new vehicle or card.

**Trigger:** Player collects 1000 coins (hypothetical). Garage notification appears: "New vehicle unlocked: Sport Car."

**Reveal (2–3 seconds):** Unlock animation. New vehicle appears in the garage with stats (speed, handling).

**Feedback:** Satisfying orchestral sting. The new vehicle is *immediately playable* in the next run.

**Integration (5+ minutes):** Player visits garage, equips new vehicle for next run. Feels like meaningful progression.

**Frequency:** Low (once every 3–5 runs for casual players; sooner for dedicated players).

**Dopamine release:** Medium. Unlocks validate long-term play ("I'm progressing!").

**Health:** Very healthy. No grinding required; unlocks are natural by-product of playing. No time-gating or FOMO.

## Reward Cadence Table

| Loop Type | Frequency | Reward Type | Randomness | Agency | Consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro (action) | Every 2–5s | Sensory feedback only | None | High | Automatic |
| Minute (delivery) | Every 30–45s | Coins + combo multiplier | None (skill-based) | High | Clear |
| Session (run end) | Every 45–90s | Cosmetic unlock (rare) | None | High | Clear |

**Exploit-risk:** All loops are low-risk. No hidden odds, no scarcity language, no daily login penalties.

## Combo Multiplier as Dopamine Engine

The **combo multiplier** (capped 8×) is the primary dopamine hook:

**Anticipation phase:** After a clean delivery, player sees combo counter at ×2, ×3, etc. Thought: "If I get one more clean delivery, I hit ×4!" This *anticipation* is the dopamine spike.

**Tension phase:** Next delivery is slightly harder (tighter time limit, more traffic). Player is hyper-focused on executing cleanly. Risk is real.

**Resolution:** If player succeeds, combo increments. Chime plays. Anticipation primes for ×5. If player crashes, combo resets to ×1. Player feels the loss but can immediately retry (instant restart).

**Why healthy:** Combo is earned through *skill and timing*, not chance. The player controls whether to push for ×8 (risky) or settle for current combo (safe). This is agency, not manipulation.

---

## Absence of Exploitative Loops

**No daily login bonus:** Players are never penalized for missing a day.

**No limited-time cosmetics:** All unlocks are permanent and earnable through normal play.

**No loot boxes:** All rewards are predictable and transparent.

**No streak counter:** Combo resets on crash, but no guilt mechanic ("don't lose your streak!"). Each run is fresh.

**No pay-to-skip:** All cosmetics and vehicles are earnable with regular play. No time-gates.
