# 13 — Retention Strategy: Delivery Rush

## Overview
Delivery Rush retention is built on **intrinsic mastery loops**, not coercive mechanics. Players return because:
1. **Combo chasing** is addictive (anticipation + risk/reward).
2. **Vehicle/card progression** gives long-term goals.
3. **Personal best records** encourage speedrunning and skill refinement.
4. **Optional competitive hooks** (leaderboards) let players measure mastery.

No daily streaks, no FOMO events, no FOMO language. If players leave, the core loop wasn't engaging enough—that's a design problem, not a retention problem.

---

## Intrinsic Hooks

### Hook 1: Combo Mastery
**What it is:** Chaining consecutive clean deliveries to reach ×8 multiplier (cap). Each combo level requires harder execution (tighter time limits, more traffic).

**Why return:** "Can I hit ×8 combo consistently?" Player is chasing a skill ceiling.

**Depth:** Achievable quickly (first-time players might hit ×3–4), but ×8 requires real practice. Players spend runs optimizing routes and throttle timing to keep combo alive.

**Early game:** Hitting ×3 combo feels like achievement. Player feels capable.

**Mid game:** ×5–6 become targets. Player is deliberately practicing throttle timing at intersections.

**Late game:** ×8 is the mastery cap. Reaching it even once is memorable. Reaching it consistently (3+ times in one session) signals true expertise.

---

### Hook 2: Vehicle Progression
**What it is:** Unlocking Starter → Sport → Super → Hyper via coin accumulation.

**Why return:** "Unlock the next vehicle tier."

**Early game:** New vehicle every 3–5 runs. Feels frequent and rewarding. Each vehicle *feels* different (noticeable speed increase, different handling).

**Mid game:** Super vehicle (~5000 coins) is the hard gate. Takes 6–8 casual runs or 2–3 dedicated runs to afford.

**Late game:** Hyper vehicle (~8000 coins) is an aspirational goal. Players work toward it over multiple sessions.

**Depth:** Vehicles are horizontal progression (faster, not strictly stronger). They don't trivialize the game; they just feel snappier. Skill remains paramount.

---

### Hook 3: Card Experimentation
**What it is:** Equippable modifier cards (Speed Boost, Coin Magnifier, Traffic Reduction, Shield). Players mix and match 3 cards per run.

**Why return:** "Try different card combinations."

**Early game:** Discovery. "What does Shield do?" Player experiments with each card's effect.

**Mid game:** Optimization. "Speed Boost + Coin Magnifier + Traffic Reduction." Player finds synergies and builds for different goals (high score vs. survival).

**Late game:** Mastery builds. "This card combo lets me maintain ×8 combo more easily." Player develops personal strategies.

---

### Hook 4: Speedrunning & Personal Bests
**What it is:** Personal-best tracking (score, combo, coins, deliveries-per-run). Optional leaderboard for global best scores.

**Why return:** "Beat my personal best."

**Mechanics:**
- Personal best score: Recorded per session/globally (tracked locally).
- "Personal Best: 4250 coins in one run" displays in the results screen.
- Optional global leaderboard shows top 100 players this week (optional feature; no pay-to-progress).

**Depth:** Endless ceiling. Score-chasing appeals to competitive players and perfectionists. Speedrunning (completing X deliveries in minimal time) is secondary challenge.

---

### Hook 5: Discovery & Cosmetics
**What it is:** Optional cosmetics (car colors, wheels, decals, engine effects) earned through play and a cosmetic shop.

**Why return:** "Unlock the next cosmetic tier."

**Pacing:** Cosmetics are frequent (every 2–3 runs). Variety is high (20+ cosmetic options, combining colors/wheels/decals = 100s of appearances).

**Late game:** Cosmetics are primarily end-game content. Players who've unlocked all vehicles collect cosmetics and customize their appearance.

## Retention Metrics & Targets

### D-Cohort Targets for Delivery Rush

| Metric | Definition | Target | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Players return within 1 day | 45–55% | Arcade loop is snackable ("one more run"). High D1 indicates core loop works. |
| **D7** | Players return within 7 days | 25–35% | Vehicle progression keeps players engaged; some drop off after first vehicle unlock. |
| **D30** | Players return within 30 days | 12–20% | All vehicles unlocked, cosmetics are main hook. Hardcore players remain; casuals taper. |
| **WAU** | Weekly active users | 20–30% of D7 | Many D7 players play multiple times per week; some are weekend-only. |
| **MAU** | Monthly active users | 30–50% of D30 | Some D30 players play once or twice per month; core audience is more frequent. |

**Interpretation:**
- D1 > 45% = Core loop hooks players immediately (good sign).
- D7 > 25% = Vehicle progression + combo chasing keeps players engaged for a week.
- D30 > 12% = Long-tail appeal; some players return monthly for cosmetics or high-score chasing.

## Progression Arc (First 30 Days)

### Days 1–3 (Novelty Phase)
**Hook:** "This game is fun and easy to learn."

- Player unlocks Starter vehicle (immediate).
- First attempts at ×3–4 combo feel like mastery.
- Cosmetics appear naturally; player customizes appearance.
- Personal best is set (probably 800–1200 coins).

**Return drivers:** "I want to beat that score."

---

### Days 4–7 (Progression Phase)
**Hook:** "I can unlock the Sport car."

- Player earns 1500+ coins (reaches Sport unlock).
- Sport vehicle feels meaningfully faster; motivation spikes.
- Attempts at ×5 combo begin.
- Player starts to understand card synergies.

**Return drivers:** "Unlock the next vehicle. Beat my personal best."

---

### Days 8–14 (Mastery Phase)
**Hook:** "I'm getting *really* good at this."

- Super vehicle is now a visible goal (3500 coins, ~5–7 runs away).
- Player consistently hits ×5–6 combo; ×7–8 are occasional peaks.
- Speedrunning becomes secondary challenge (completing 8+ deliveries per run).
- Personal best climbs steadily as player optimizes routes.

**Return drivers:** "Reach ×8 combo. Grind for Super vehicle."

---

### Days 15–30 (Expert Phase)
**Hook:** "I'm competing against myself and the leaderboard."

- Hyper vehicle (8000 coins) is a long-term goal.
- Player regularly hits ×7–8 combo.
- Cosmetic collecting replaces vehicle hunger.
- Leaderboard entries become motivator (if available).
- Some players speedrun specific routes for time records.

**Return drivers:** "Beat my personal best. Reach Hyper vehicle. Collect cosmetics."

---

## Absence of Coercive Hooks

**No daily login bonuses.** Players are never penalized for missing a day. If they return after a week, the game is as fresh as ever.

**No limited-time events.** No seasonal cosmetics that vanish. All cosmetics are permanent and earnable.

**No battle pass.** No progress reset mechanics that create sunk-cost anxiety.

**No streak counter.** Combo resets naturally; no guilt mechanic.

**No pay-to-skip grind.** All vehicles and cosmetics are earnable through normal play. No time-gates or paywalls.

---

## Catch-Up Mechanics

**For players who take a 1-week break:**

A returning player who plays 3 runs might earn 2000 coins. They can:
- Spend on a card they haven't tried.
- Get 1/4 of the way to a vehicle unlock (no feeling of having "fallen behind").

**No asymmetric grinding.** Coin earning rate is consistent; returning players catch up naturally.

---

## Validation Checklist

- [x] Intrinsic hooks identified and compelling (combo chasing, vehicle progression, personal bests, cosmetics, discovery).
- [x] Coercive mechanics are absent (no daily streaks, no limited-time events, no FOMO).
- [x] D1/D7/D30 targets are realistic (45%+ D1, 25%+ D7, 12%+ D30 for arcade genre).
- [x] Players who miss 3–7 days can catch up without grinding (coin earn rate is consistent).
- [x] No mechanic punishes players for missing days (no progress loss, no streak penalty).
- [x] Competitive/social hooks exist (optional leaderboard, personal-best tracking).
- [x] Late-game progression is visible (cosmetics indefinite, card variations, speedrun challenges).
- [x] No FOMO language in core onboarding ("don't miss this" is absent).
