## 15-Second Hook

Delivery Rush is a casual arcade courier game for mobile. You play as a delivery driver; the twist is that every order completed raises a multiplier (up to 8×) that boosts your coin payout, but one crash or missed delivery resets it to 1. Unlike other driving games, this one rewards planning and throttle timing over reflexes. It's arcade speed meets delivery tension—"Crazy Taxi meets Crossy Road"—and you can play a full run in 60 seconds.

## 60-Second Pitch

Delivery Rush is a casual arcade courier game built for players chasing the next high score.

**Setting:** You're a delivery driver navigating a bright, procedurally generated low-poly 3D city, regenerated fresh each run (daily seed). No two runs look the same.

**Core loop:** Hold the center button to accelerate (hold longer = go faster), release to brake. Tap left or right to queue a turn (commits at the next intersection). Drive to a pulsing pickup beacon, grab the order, then reach the dropoff before the timer expires. Each completed delivery adds 7 seconds to your run clock and coins to your score, multiplied by your combo. Crash or miss an order, and the multiplier resets to 1×. The combo caps at 8×. Repeat until time runs out.

**Identity:** Bright, clean low-poly 3D (think Crossy Road). Responsive touch controls (one-thumb friendly). Synthesized audio—upbeat beeps and chimes. Emoji order glyphs. Colorblind-distinct button UI. Zero story friction.

**Why now:** Post-Crossy Road, the casual low-poly 3D market is proven. Hyper-casual driving (Cars: Fast as Lightning) sustains millions of plays via ad-supported monetization. Procedural cities reduce art burden, enabling solo teams. Combo mechanics (borrowed from Crazy Taxi, popularized by roguelikes) drive engagement. Delivery Rush merges these: arcade driving + throttle skill + combo economy + zero friction = a gap in 2026.

**Business:** Free-to-play web (portrait mobile-first). Cosmetic garage upgrades (soft currency). Rewarded video (2× coins). Premium $14.99 SKU planned post-alpha. We're solo/AI-assisted, targeting alpha playability at 60 fps on mid-range phones by Q3 2026. Projected reach: 100K+ monthly active players in year one (based on hyper-casual benchmarks).

## 3-Minute Pitch (Outline)

**Talking points:**
1. **Market gap:** Casual arcade + low-poly 3D + combo mechanics + one-thumb throttle controls. Hyper-casual space is proven (Crossy Road, Alto's); combo economy is proven (Crazy Taxi, roguelikes). No one has merged all four in this control scheme.
2. **Core loop and skill ceiling:** Throttle timing is approachable (hold = go) and deep (when to brake mid-turn). Every run is a fresh difficulty puzzle (traffic density, order timers, city layout).
3. **Tech enabler:** Three.js rewrite eliminates Phaser's per-frame vector-redraw FPS tanking. Static geometry + instancing = 60 fps target on mobile.
4. **Retention proof:** Prototype (Phaser 2D) tested with players shows "one more run" momentum. No engagement cliffs. Combo reset creates natural retry loops.
5. **Post-launch:** Cosmetic vehicles/cards, seasonal daily seeds, potential Switch port (gyro steering).
